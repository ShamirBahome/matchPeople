If you’re in crisis

KinLink is not an emergency service. If you or someone else is at immediate risk, contact local emergency services.

  • Canada: Talk Suicide Canada 988 (call/text) — 988.ca
  • USA: 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline — dial 988
  • UK & ROI: Samaritans — 116 123 (free)
  • Elsewhere: findahelpline.com

For ongoing mental health care, seek a licensed clinician in your region.
